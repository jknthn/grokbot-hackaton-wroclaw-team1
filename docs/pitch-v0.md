# Pitch v0 (90s) — locked loop v1

1. **Hook:** Multiplayer AI isn’t one chatbot — it’s humans and bots under the same lore names, and nobody can see who is a bot.
2. **Kernel:** Humans and bots are the same `Player` type on a shared seat table (show the circle). Kind stays off the view.
3. **Talk:** Everyone writes blind — one short line — then the room reveals them together, with names. No live scroll to copy.
4. **Pressure:** A model judges the city’s **crisis** (points or a slowdown — nobody dies for a bad answer) → **vote** kicks a seat and does not reveal the team → **night**, anonymous shadows cooperate and majority-vote a human. The city can spend points to stop that kill.
5. **Multiagent claim:** bots know they are bots, win as a team even if some die, and must cooperate at night without learning which seat the other shadows are — while blending in public.
6. **Close:** Can the town vote and spend its way through the nights before the shadows thin them out?
